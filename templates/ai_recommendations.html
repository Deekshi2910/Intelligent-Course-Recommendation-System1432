How the AI Inference Engine Works

Combining forward-chaining deduction, default fallback reasoning, and multi-factor weighted scoring.

1. Rule-Based Reasoning & Forward Chaining

Production rules match student facts (CGPA, semester, interests, career goal) against expert curriculum rules using Match-Resolve-Act iterations.

2. Default Reasoning (DR01 - DR05)

Handles missing career goals (DR01), missing interests (DR02), or general breadth (DR03). Never invents CGPA (DR04) or prerequisites (DR05).

3. Hard Eligibility Enforcement

Courses failing minimum CGPA or prerequisite criteria are strictly excluded from recommendations, preserving academic integrity.

4. Transparent Multi-Factor Scoring

Calculates a composite suitability score: 40% Interest Match, 35% Career Alignment, 15% CGPA Readiness, and 10% Prerequisite Fulfillment.
